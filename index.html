Why Nexus X1 Ultra

Built for those who
refuse to settle

01
AMOLED ProMax Display

6.9" QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED with 3000 nits peak brightness, 1-144Hz adaptive refresh and a pixel density of 512 PPI — colours so vivid they seem lit from within.

02
Phantom X9 Processor

Our custom 3nm octa-core chip with a dedicated 40-TOPS AI neural engine. Handles real-time translation, computational photography and on-device LLMs simultaneously.

03
8K Cinematic Video

Record at 8K@30fps or 4K@120fps with optical image stabilisation across all lenses. A 24mm f/1.6 aperture lets you capture night scenes as the eye sees them.

04
100W HyperCharge

From 0% to full in 37 minutes. 50W wireless MagCharge. 10W reverse wireless charging. The 6000 mAh silicon-carbon battery outlasts a full day of heavy use, every time.

05
Titanium Armour Shell

Grade-5 aerospace titanium frame with Corning Gorilla Armor 3 glass front and back. IP68 dust and water resistant to 8 metres for 30 minutes — built for the real world.

06
Nexus AI Assistant

An on-device 7B parameter model runs entirely offline. Summaries, voice commands, scene recognition, real-time translation in 62 languages — privately, without touching a server.
